Official Rule Specification: Triad Strike
-----------------------------------------

Triad Strike is a high-speed, strategic field sport defined by its dynamic neutral defense and built-in comeback mechanics.

### 1\. Roster & Team Structure

-   **The Odd-Number Requirement:** Total player count ($N$) on the field must always be an odd number (e.g., 5, 7, or 9 players).

-   **The Teams:** Active players are divided into two equal opposing factions: **Team A** and **Team B**.

-   **Neutral Defenders:** The remaining players (1 player in smaller games, or 3 players in larger games) serve as independent, neutral defenders. They do not belong to either team; their sole objective is to disrupt gameplay, steal the ball, and guard both goals.

### 2\. General Ball Handling & Body Mechanics

-   **Standard Players (Teams A & B):** May only use their **feet, calves, and knees** to contact the ball.

    -   *The Air-Launch Exception:* A player may use their hands exclusively to toss the ball vertically into the air to set up an immediate volley kick or knee-strike. The ball cannot touch the ground between the toss and the kick.

-   **Neutral Defenders:** May use their **feet, calves, shoulders, head, and arms (strictly below the elbow)**. No hand contact is permitted.

### 3\. The Goal Zone

-   **Spatial Dimension:** A semi-circular perimeter extending exactly 3 feet forward from the baseline of each goal.

-   **Zone Permissions:** Only the attacking team's standard players are permitted to enter and hold the ball within this 3-foot zone to setup a shot. Neutral defenders must remain outside this perimeter to guard the goal.

### 4\. Game Starts & Possession Mechanics

```
[Team A Scores] ➔ [Team B Scores] ➔ Match Resets to Neutral Face-off
[Team A Scores] ➔ [Team A Scores] ➔ Team B Awards "True Possession" (Comeback Advantage)

```

-   **Neutral Face-off:** At the start of the game or after alternating scores, the ball is placed at midfield. All players must stand an equal distance away from the ball before play is whistled live.

-   **True Possession (Comeback Mechanic):** If Team A scores **2 consecutive goals** without Team B scoring in between, Team B is awarded True Possession for the reset. The game resets at midfield, but one player from Team B starts directly adjacent to the ball, while all other players must stand back at a designated distance.

### 5\. Penalty Regulations

-   **Infractions:** Triggered by illegal body contact, handballs outside of designated exceptions, or a neutral defender entering the 3-foot Goal Zone.

-   **The Penalty Set:** The fouled team receives a free kick. To defend the shot, the **Neutral Defenders plus 2 players from the penalised team** form a defensive wall.
